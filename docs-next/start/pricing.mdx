Use **Administration → Usage** to inspect the active billing window and the consumption reported for your organization. Before expanding a rollout, estimate volume from a representative production period rather than a short development run.

<Tip>
  Separate environments such as `development`, `staging`, and `production`. This makes dashboards, audits, and usage investigations easier to scope.
</Tip>
