This is the first of five steps that take you from nothing to a deployed policy. Nothing
here needs a sales call, and the whole path takes about ten minutes.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your organization">
  Go to **[befailproof.ai/get-started](https://befailproof.ai/get-started/)** and create an
  organization. You get an **org slug** — every URL in the dashboard is scoped to it, like
  `app.befailproof.ai/<org-slug>/sessions`.
</Step>

<Step title="Sign in">
  Sign-in is passwordless. Enter your email, and the dashboard mails you a six-digit
  one-time passcode. There is no password to store, rotate, or leak.

  Membership decides who gets in. Once you are in, invite the rest of your team from
  **admin → Users**, which mails them the same one-time passcode.
</Step>

<Step title="Create your first key">
  Open **admin → Keys** and create a key with two permissions:

  | Permission | What it lets the key do |
  |---|---|
  | `events:add` | Send what your agents did **up** to your organization |
  | `policies:pull` | Receive centrally-managed policy **down** onto a machine |

  Those two are the whole [machine key](/es/cloud/admin#the-three-keys-most-teams-need).
  Every step that follows uses it.

  <Warning>
    The secret is displayed **once**, at creation. Copy it now and put it somewhere your
    team can find it. If you lose it, regenerate the key — you cannot read it back.
  </Warning>
</Step>

</Steps>

---

## Why those two permissions and no others

A key that can report activity does not need to read anything, and a key that receives
policy does not need to change it. Scoping to exactly `events:add` + `policies:pull` means
a key sitting on a laptop or a CI runner cannot read your organization's data, mint more
keys, or edit a policy — even if it leaks.

The two halves are also verified independently at connect time, so a key carrying one and
not the other fails with a precise reason instead of half-working. The full catalogue, and
the read-only and admin keys you will want later, are on
[Access and permissions](/es/cloud/admin).
