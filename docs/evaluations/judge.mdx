---
title: "Hosted LLM judges are retired"
description: "Read historical judge scores and choose code, Jev, or written analysis for new work."
icon: "scale"
---

Hosted LLM judge evaluations can no longer be created or run. Existing definitions
and historical scores remain readable; unfinished judge runs are cancelled.

For new evaluations, use [code](/evaluations/write) to count and compare, or a
[Jev classifier](/evaluations/jev) for questions with defined answers. Use the
[assistant](/sessions/assistant) or an audit when you need a written explanation.

Custom evaluations in [your own worker](/reference/evaluator-sdk) remain supported,
including those that call models you manage yourself.
