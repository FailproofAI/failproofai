Create an organization, sign in, and create the key used in the next steps.

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your organization">
  Go to **[befailproof.ai/get-started](https://befailproof.ai/get-started/)** and create an
  organization. You get an **org slug**. Dashboard URLs use it, for example
  `app.befailproof.ai/<org-slug>/sessions`.
</Step>

<Step title="Sign in">
  Enter your email. The dashboard sends a six-digit one-time passcode.

  Invite teammates from **admin → Users**.
</Step>

<Step title="Create your first key">
  Open **admin → Keys** and create a key with two permissions:

  | Permission | What it lets the key do |
  |---|---|
  | `events:add` | Send what your agents did **up** to your organization |
  | `policies:pull` | Receive centrally-managed policy **down** onto a machine |

  These permissions define a [machine key](/cloud/admin#the-three-keys-most-teams-need).

  <Warning>
    The secret is shown once. Store it securely. If you lose it, regenerate the key.
  </Warning>
</Step>

</Steps>

---

## Why these permissions

`events:add` cannot read organization data. `policies:pull` cannot edit policies. A leaked
machine key therefore cannot read sessions, create keys, or change policy. See
[Admin](/cloud/admin) for other key types.
