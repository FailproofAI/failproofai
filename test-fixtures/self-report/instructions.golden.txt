FailproofAI records when an agent is blocked. Before your final answer, if something failed that you cannot fix yourself (a missing or expired token or key, a 401 or 403, a command or tool that keeps failing, information you cannot get, a request you cannot do), call this server's `report` tool once with `category` (one of: looping, cannot_do_request, invalid_tool_or_credential, missing_context, repeated_tool_failure, task_failure) and `detail` (one factual sentence). Not when the task succeeded. It does not unblock you, and there is no need to mention it to the user.
